## Cụm từ

- **glued to their phones** — dán mắt vào điện thoại — At family dinners, teenagers are often glued to their phones instead of talking.
- **screen time** — thời gian dùng màn hình — Doctors recommend limiting screen time for children under five to one hour a day.
- **face-to-face communication** — giao tiếp trực tiếp — Video calls are convenient, but face-to-face communication builds stronger relationships.
- **at the touch of a button** — chỉ với một cú bấm — Today we can order food, pay bills and book flights at the touch of a button.
- **a wealth of information** — lượng thông tin phong phú — The internet offers a wealth of information, but not all of it is reliable.
- **fake news** — tin giả — Fake news about the storm spread faster on Facebook than the official warnings.
- **personal data** — dữ liệu cá nhân — Apps often collect personal data such as our location and contacts.
- **breach of privacy** — vi phạm quyền riêng tư — Selling customers' phone numbers to advertisers is a clear breach of privacy.
- **replace human workers** — thay thế lao động con người — Self-service kiosks have started to replace human workers in fast-food restaurants.
- **automate repetitive tasks** — tự động hoá việc lặp đi lặp lại — Banks use software to automate repetitive tasks like checking forms.
- **become obsolete** — trở nên lỗi thời — Some people argue that printed dictionaries have become obsolete.
- **the digital divide** — khoảng cách số (giữa người có và không có công nghệ) — The digital divide became obvious when rural pupils had no laptop for online classes.
- **addicted to** — nghiện — Many children are addicted to short videos and struggle to put their phones down.
- **keep in touch** — giữ liên lạc — Messaging apps make it easy to keep in touch with friends who live abroad.
- **a double-edged sword** — con dao hai lưỡi — Artificial intelligence is a double-edged sword: it saves time but may also cost jobs.
- **handwriting skills** — kỹ năng viết tay — Typing on keyboards all day may weaken children's handwriting skills.
- **a free public service** — dịch vụ công miễn phí — Libraries are a free public service that everyone can use regardless of income.
- **improve efficiency** — tăng hiệu quả — Robots on assembly lines improve efficiency and reduce errors.
- **tech-savvy** — rành công nghệ — My grandmother is surprisingly tech-savvy and pays for everything with QR codes.
- **misuse of technology** — lạm dụng công nghệ — The misuse of technology, such as deepfake videos, is a growing concern.

## Kho ý tưởng Task 2

### Ít người viết tay

Đề (03/09/2026; 16/03, Two-part): Fewer people write by hand. Why? Is this a positive or negative development?

**Lý do**
- Typing is faster and easier to edit → e.g. a student can type 50 words a minute but write perhaps 20.
- Work and study have moved online → assignments, forms and messages are all digital → e.g. universities now ask for essays to be submitted as files.

**Tích cực**
- Efficiency → documents are easy to share, search and store → e.g. a whole team can edit one Google Doc at the same time.

**Tiêu cực**
- Memory and learning → writing by hand helps people remember information → e.g. students who take notes on paper often recall lectures better.
- Loss of a personal touch → a handwritten card shows effort → e.g. a birthday card from a grandparent means more than a text message.
- Exam disadvantage → people who rarely write by hand struggle in paper-based exams.

### Điện thoại làm mất kỹ năng giao tiếp trực tiếp

Đề (11/07/2026, Opinion): More people use phones and computers, so they are losing face-to-face communication skills. Agree or disagree?

**Đồng ý**
- Less practice → people choose texting over calling or meeting → e.g. some young people avoid phone calls and even order taxis without speaking.
- Distraction during real conversations → people are glued to their phones at the table → e.g. friends sitting in a café, each scrolling separately.
- Missing body language → emojis cannot replace tone and facial expression → e.g. a short reply like "ok" is easily misunderstood.

**Không đồng ý**
- Technology also connects → people keep in touch with relatives abroad via video calls → e.g. a family in Hà Nội talking weekly to a son in Japan.
- Online practice can build confidence → shy people practise expressing ideas in group chats and online classes first.

### Thư viện công cộng: đáng đầu tư hay lãng phí?

Đề (04/07/2026; 24/06; 13/08, Discussion): Governments should fund free public libraries vs. libraries are a waste because of the internet. Discuss both views.

**Lãng phí**
- Most information is online → people search rather than borrow → e.g. a student checks an online encyclopedia in seconds.
- High running costs → buildings, staff and books cost money that could go to digital services → e.g. an e-library app serves the whole country at once.

**Nên đầu tư**
- Equal access → not everyone has a computer or fast internet → e.g. children from poor families do homework in libraries.
- Quiet study space → many homes are crowded and noisy → e.g. university students queue for library seats before exams.
- Community role → free classes, reading clubs, help for elderly people learning to use computers.
- Reliable sources → librarians help people find trustworthy information instead of fake news.

> Gợi ý: kết luận: thư viện nên đổi mới (wifi, sách số, không gian học), không nên đóng cửa.

### Sách in còn cần thiết?

Đề (03/06 & 06/06/2026, Discussion): Printed books are no longer needed in the digital era vs. they still matter. Discuss both views.

**Không cần nữa**
- Convenience → one device can carry hundreds of books → e.g. a Kindle weighs less than a single paperback.
- Cheaper and greener → no paper, printing or transport → e.g. e-books often cost half the price of the print version.

**Vẫn cần**
- Better for reading and memory → less eye strain and fewer distractions than a phone screen → e.g. no notifications pop up in a paper book.
- Durability → printed books do not need batteries, updates or internet → e.g. a 50-year-old book can still be read today.
- Emotional and cultural value → gifts, collections, signed copies.

### Robot: quan trọng hay nguy hiểm?

Đề (06/06/2026, Discussion): Robots are important for our future vs. robots are dangerous. Discuss both views.

**Quan trọng**
- Dangerous and repetitive work → robots can work in mines, on chemical sites or in disaster zones → e.g. robots searching collapsed buildings after earthquakes.
- Ageing populations → robots can help care for elderly people → e.g. Japan uses robots to lift patients in care homes.
- Productivity → factories improve efficiency and cut errors.

**Nguy hiểm**
- Job losses → robots replace human workers in factories and shops → e.g. a car plant may need only a few hundred workers instead of thousands.
- Safety and control → faulty or hacked machines can cause accidents → e.g. self-driving car crashes.
- Over-dependence → people may lose basic skills if machines do everything.

### Công ty internet giữ dữ liệu cá nhân

Đề (S9, 2026, Adv–Disadv): Large internet companies hold people's personal information. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

**Lợi ích**
- Personalised services → maps, music and shopping apps give better suggestions → e.g. a map app predicts your route home at 5 pm.
- Free services → data and advertising pay for email and social media.

**Bất lợi**
- Privacy risks → data leaks expose millions of users → e.g. stolen phone numbers lead to scam calls.
- Manipulation → targeted ads and political messages can influence opinions.
- Lack of control → users rarely read the terms and do not know who sees their data.

> Gợi ý: nhiều người chọn "disadvantages outweigh", với điều kiện đề xuất luật bảo vệ dữ liệu chặt hơn.

## Speaking dự đoán

**Which websites do you often visit? (Part 1 – Websites)**

Mostly news sites and YouTube, to be honest. I usually **skim the headlines** on VnExpress while having breakfast, and then in the evening I watch a few English lessons on YouTube. I also use online dictionaries **a great deal** now that I'm preparing for IELTS. I'd say they're the sites I **can't do without**.

**Do you use a computer or a tablet more? (Part 1 – Computers/Tablets)**

Definitely a computer, because I need it for work. I spend **the best part of** eight hours a day on my laptop, writing reports and answering emails. A tablet is great for **lying on the sofa** and watching films, but it's not very practical for typing. So my tablet mostly **gathers dust** these days.

**Do you prefer reading on paper or on screen? (Part 1 – Paper)**

On paper, without a doubt. When I read on screen, I **get distracted** by notifications and I find myself switching between apps. With a printed book I can **lose myself in the story** for an hour. Also, my eyes **get tired** much more quickly after a long day looking at a monitor.

**Can technology replace language teachers? (Part 3 – Person)**

I don't think it can **completely replace** them, at least not in the near future. Apps and AI tools are great for **drilling vocabulary** and checking grammar, and they're available 24/7. But a good teacher can **spot exactly** why a student keeps making the same mistake and can keep them motivated when they want to give up. I think the best approach is a **combination of both**.

**Will robots replace shop assistants? (Part 3 – Experience)**

In some places, it's **already happening**. Supermarkets have self-checkout machines, and many cafés let you order on a screen. However, for things like clothes or electronics, people still want **personal advice** from someone who knows the products. So my guess is that robots will **take over** simple, repetitive jobs, while human staff will focus on customer service.

**Are people less patient nowadays because of technology? (Part 3 – Experience)**

Yes, I'd say so. We're used to **instant results**: next-day delivery, instant messages, videos that load in a second. So when something takes a bit longer, like waiting in a queue or for someone to reply, people **lose their patience** very quickly. I've noticed it in myself, actually; if a web page doesn't load in three seconds, I **give up on it**.
