## Cụm từ

- **public spending** — chi tiêu công — Healthcare and education take up the largest share of public spending.
- **allocate funds to** — phân bổ ngân sách cho — The government should allocate funds to rural hospitals before building new stadiums.
- **taxpayers' money** — tiền thuế của dân — Many people feel that sending astronauts to Mars is a waste of taxpayers' money.
- **pressing issues** — vấn đề cấp bách — Poverty and clean water are pressing issues that cannot wait.
- **space exploration** — khám phá vũ trụ — Space exploration has given us satellite navigation and better weather forecasts.
- **scientific breakthroughs** — đột phá khoa học — Many scientific breakthroughs, such as mRNA vaccines, came from years of public funding.
- **the profit motive** — động cơ lợi nhuận — Under the profit motive, drug companies may ignore diseases that mainly affect poor countries.
- **the gap between rich and poor** — khoảng cách giàu nghèo — The gap between rich and poor has widened in many fast-growing cities.
- **welfare system** — hệ thống phúc lợi — A strong welfare system protects people who lose their jobs or fall ill.
- **a safety net** — lưới an sinh — Unemployment benefits act as a safety net for workers who are laid off.
- **an ageing population** — dân số già hoá — With an ageing population, Vietnam will need far more pension funding by 2040.
- **save for retirement** — tiết kiệm cho tuổi nghỉ hưu — Young people rarely save for retirement because it seems so far away.
- **affordable housing** — nhà ở giá phải chăng — Rising land prices have made affordable housing almost impossible to find in Hanoi.
- **get on the property ladder** — mua được căn nhà đầu tiên — It now takes an office worker over 20 years of saving to get on the property ladder.
- **a sense of dependency** — tâm lý ỷ lại — Critics claim free housing could create a sense of dependency on the state.
- **social inequality** — bất bình đẳng xã hội — Social inequality often leads to lower trust and higher crime.
- **international cooperation** — hợp tác quốc tế — Problems such as pandemics require international cooperation to solve.
- **long-term investment** — khoản đầu tư dài hạn — Funding basic research is a long-term investment that may pay off decades later.
- **a fairer distribution of wealth** — phân phối của cải công bằng hơn — Higher taxes on luxury goods could lead to a fairer distribution of wealth.

## Kho ý tưởng Task 2

### Chi tiền cho khám phá vũ trụ

Đề (23/07/2026; 03/2026, Opinion): Money spent on space exploration is not justified; it should be spent on problems on Earth. Agree or disagree?

**Đồng ý**
- Pressing issues come first → hunger, disease and poverty need money now → e.g. the cost of one rocket launch could build dozens of rural schools.
- Benefits are uncertain and slow → many missions produce knowledge with no practical use for decades.

**Không đồng ý**
- Space research solves problems on Earth → e.g. satellites give storm warnings that save lives in Vietnam's typhoon season; GPS powers delivery and ride-hailing apps.
- Spin-off technologies → e.g. water purification, scratch-resistant lenses and medical imaging came from space programmes.
- The budget is small → space usually takes well under 1% of a national budget, so cutting it would not end poverty.

> Gợi ý: hướng phổ biến: disagree, nhưng thừa nhận nên ưu tiên dự án có ích thực tế (vệ tinh, khí hậu).

### Doanh nghiệp tư nhân tài trợ nghiên cứu

Đề (17/03/2026, Adv–Disadv): Private companies, not governments, now fund most scientific research. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

**Lợi ích**
- More money and faster results → companies invest heavily to bring products to market → e.g. tech firms spend billions a year on AI research.
- Less burden on public budgets → governments can focus on healthcare and education.

**Bất lợi**
- The profit motive decides research → unprofitable but important areas are ignored → e.g. treatments for tropical diseases receive little attention.
- Less transparency → results may be kept secret or presented selectively → e.g. a company may hide negative test results about a product.
- Patents raise prices → life-saving drugs become too expensive for poor patients.

### Nhà ở miễn phí cho người không đủ khả năng

Đề (12/09/2026, Opinion): Governments should provide free housing for everyone who cannot afford it. Agree or disagree?

**Đồng ý**
- Housing is a basic need → without a stable home, people cannot keep a job or send children to school.
- Reduces wider costs → homelessness leads to health problems and crime that the state pays for anyway.

**Không đồng ý**
- Enormous cost → e.g. building enough flats for every low-income family in big cities would take a huge share of the budget.
- A sense of dependency → people may lose motivation to improve their income.
- Better alternatives → affordable housing schemes, rent support or low-interest loans → e.g. Vietnam's social housing programme sells flats below market price instead of giving them away.

### Thu hẹp khoảng cách giàu nghèo

Đề (16/08/2026, Opinion): Reducing the gap between rich and poor is the best way to create a happier society. Agree or disagree?

**Đồng ý**
- Less inequality, more trust → societies with a smaller gap often have lower crime and better health → e.g. Nordic countries top happiness rankings.
- Equal opportunity → poor children can access good schools and healthcare.

**Không đồng ý (một phần)**
- Happiness depends on more than money → family, health, free time and community matter → e.g. some low-income countries report high life satisfaction.
- Other priorities → clean air, safety and work-life balance also affect happiness.
- "Best way" is too strong → it should be one of several approaches.

### Hưu trí: chính phủ lo hay cá nhân tự tiết kiệm

Đề (01/07/2026, Discussion): Governments should support retired people vs. individuals should save for their own retirement. Discuss both views.

**Chính phủ hỗ trợ**
- Older people built the economy → they deserve a safety net.
- Not everyone can save → low-income and informal workers have little left each month → e.g. street vendors in Vietnam often have no pension at all.

**Cá nhân tự lo**
- An ageing population makes state pensions unsustainable → fewer workers support more retirees.
- Personal responsibility → people know their own needs and can plan early → e.g. saving 10% of income from the age of 25.

> Gợi ý: kết luận: chính phủ bảo đảm mức tối thiểu, cá nhân tiết kiệm thêm.

### Vấn đề cấp bách chỉ giải quyết được bằng hợp tác quốc tế

Đề (09/09/2026, Opinion): Most urgent problems can only be solved through international cooperation. Agree or disagree?

**Đồng ý**
- Global problems cross borders → climate change, pandemics and ocean plastic cannot be fixed by one country → e.g. the Paris Agreement.
- Shared knowledge and funding → e.g. vaccines during COVID-19 were developed and distributed with international support.

**Không đồng ý (một phần)**
- Many urgent problems are local → housing, traffic and unemployment depend on national policy.
- International agreements are slow → countries often fail to keep promises, so national action is needed first.

## Speaking dự đoán

**Would you like to travel into space? (Part 1 – Outer space & stars)**

Honestly, no, I don't think I'd **have the nerve**. The idea of being **stuck in a tiny capsule** for days doesn't appeal to me at all. I'd much rather watch a good documentary about it **from the comfort of my sofa**. But if it ever became as safe as flying, I might **change my mind**.

**What scientific development is important to you? (Part 1 – Science)**

I'd say smartphones, or more specifically mobile internet. It has **completely changed the way** I work and study; I can read the news, take online lessons and pay bills on one device. Medical progress like vaccines is obviously more important for humanity, but in terms of my **day-to-day life**, my phone has had **the biggest impact**.

**Should governments spend money on stadiums? (Part 3 – Event)**

Only **to a certain extent**. A modern stadium can attract international matches and **boost tourism**, which brings money to local businesses. But I think it's hard to justify spending huge amounts when some hospitals are **seriously overcrowded**. If I were in charge, I'd **put the money into** small sports centres in every district, so that ordinary people can actually use them.

**Should public buildings like libraries and museums be free? (Part 3 – Place)**

I believe they should be, at least for children and students. These places are meant to **spread knowledge**, and an entrance fee can **put off** people from poorer families. Of course, they need money to run, so perhaps the government could **cover the basic costs** and museums could charge for special exhibitions.

**Should schools teach children about laws? (Part 3 – Law)**

Yes, I think it's **essential**. Many people break rules simply because they **don't know their rights** or responsibilities, for example about traffic or online behaviour. Learning the basics at school would make young people **more responsible citizens**. It could be done through real cases rather than **dry theory**, so students actually remember it.

**Why do people find it hard to save money? (Part 3 – Experience)**

I think the main reason is that spending has become **far too easy**. With online shopping and e-wallets, you can buy something **on impulse** in a few seconds. Also, in big cities **the cost of living** keeps rising, so after rent and bills many young people have very little left. Personally, I only started saving once I **set up an automatic transfer** to a separate account every payday.
