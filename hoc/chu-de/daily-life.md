## Cụm từ

- **a creature of habit** — người sống theo nếp quen — I'm a creature of habit, so I have the same breakfast almost every day.
- **hit the snooze button** — bấm báo thức ngủ thêm — I always hit the snooze button at least twice before I actually get up.
- **grab a quick breakfast** — ăn sáng vội — Most office workers grab a quick breakfast from a street stall on the way to work.
- **eat on the go** — vừa đi vừa ăn, ăn vội — People who eat on the go rarely notice how much fried food they consume.
- **a home-cooked meal** — bữa cơm nhà nấu — After a long week, nothing beats a home-cooked meal with the family.
- **run errands** — đi làm mấy việc vặt — I usually run errands on Saturday morning, like paying bills and picking up parcels.
- **do the food shopping** — đi chợ, mua đồ ăn — We do the food shopping once a week to avoid buying things we don't need.
- **shop around** — so sánh giá nhiều nơi trước khi mua — It's worth shopping around before you buy a new phone, because prices vary a lot.
- **make impulse purchases** — mua bốc đồng — Flash sales on shopping apps make it very easy to make impulse purchases.
- **go window-shopping** — đi ngắm hàng, không mua — When I was a student, we used to go window-shopping in the mall because it was free and air-conditioned.
- **a close-knit neighbourhood** — khu phố gắn bó, thân thiết — I grew up in a close-knit neighbourhood where everyone knew each other's children.
- **get on well with the neighbours** — hoà thuận với hàng xóm — It's important to get on well with the neighbours, especially in an apartment block.
- **feel at home** — thấy thoải mái như ở nhà — It took me a few months to feel at home in my new flat.
- **keep the place tidy** — giữ nhà gọn gàng — Living in a small flat forces you to keep the place tidy.
- **first thing in the morning** — việc đầu tiên buổi sáng — Checking emails first thing in the morning puts me in a bad mood for the whole day.
- **scroll mindlessly** — lướt điện thoại vô thức — I often scroll mindlessly through videos before bed and then regret it.
- **a good night's sleep** — một giấc ngủ ngon — A good night's sleep makes a huge difference to how well I concentrate at work.
- **toss and turn** — trằn trọc — If I drink coffee after 3 pm, I toss and turn for hours.
- **recharge my batteries** — nạp lại năng lượng — A quiet Sunday at home is how I recharge my batteries for the week ahead.
- **a sedentary lifestyle** — lối sống ít vận động — Sitting at a desk for nine hours a day leads to a sedentary lifestyle.

## Kho ý tưởng Task 2

### Lối sống bận rộn làm hỏng thói quen ăn uống

Đề (05/09/2026, Cause–Solution): Busy lifestyles are having a negative effect on people's eating habits and health. What are the causes? What are the solutions?

**Nguyên nhân**
- Long working hours → people have no time or energy to cook → e.g. many office workers in Hanoi eat lunch at their desks and order dinner through delivery apps.
- Convenience food is cheap and everywhere → instant noodles, fried snacks and sugary milk tea are easier than a home-cooked meal.
- A sedentary lifestyle → people burn fewer calories but still eat large portions.

**Giải pháp**
- Employers → canteens with healthy, affordable meals and proper lunch breaks → e.g. some offices offer free fruit instead of biscuits.
- Individuals → batch cooking at the weekend so that healthy meals are ready for busy weekdays.
- Governments → clear nutrition labels and taxes on sugary drinks → e.g. the UK sugar tax led manufacturers to reduce sugar content.

### Thói quen mua sắm phụ thuộc nhất vào độ tuổi

Đề (12/09/2026, Opinion): Shopping habits are affected most by a person's age group. Agree or disagree?

**Đồng ý**
- Different needs at different stages of life → students buy cheap fashion, young parents buy baby products, retired people spend on health.
- Technology gap → younger people shop online and through livestreams, while many older people still prefer the local market where they can see and touch the goods.

**Không đồng ý**
- Income matters more → a high earner and a low earner of the same age shop in completely different ways.
- Location → people in the countryside have fewer shops and slower deliveries than city dwellers, whatever their age.
- Marketing and social media now influence every age group → e.g. many middle-aged Vietnamese now buy through TikTok Shop and Shopee.

> Gợi ý: "most" → có thể đồng ý một phần: tuổi quan trọng, nhưng thu nhập và nơi sống quan trọng ngang hoặc hơn.

### Ai cũng muốn có ô tô, TV, tủ lạnh

Đề (16/06/2026, Adv–Disadv): Nowadays everyone wants to own a car, a television and a fridge. Do the disadvantages of this development outweigh the advantages?

**Lợi ích**
- Comfort and time-saving → a fridge means people do the food shopping once a week instead of every day.
- Economic growth → demand for household goods creates factory and retail jobs.
- Access to information and entertainment → TV brings news and education to rural homes.

**Bất lợi**
- Environmental cost → more cars mean more emissions and traffic; old appliances become electronic waste.
- Debt and pressure → families buy on instalments to keep up with the neighbours.
- Health → more time in front of the TV and less walking.

### Chất lượng sống thế kỷ 21 tốt hơn trước

Đề (20/09/2026; 08/08, Opinion): People in the 21st century have a better quality of life than people in previous centuries. Agree or disagree?

**Đồng ý**
- Longer, healthier lives → vaccines and modern hospitals → e.g. life expectancy in Vietnam has risen from around 60 in the 1970s to over 73 today.
- Material comfort → clean water, electricity and household appliances save hours of hard work every day.
- Opportunities → education and travel are open to far more people.

**Không đồng ý (một phần)**
- New problems → stress, long working hours and loneliness, especially in big cities.
- Poor sleep and mental health → people are always connected to work through their phones.
- Pollution → air quality in many Asian cities is worse than a few decades ago.

### Mua sắm trực tuyến thay cho cửa hàng

Đề (đề quen thuộc, Positive–Negative): More and more people shop online rather than in physical shops. Is this a positive or negative development?

**Tích cực**
- Convenience and price → customers can shop around in minutes and get goods delivered to the door.
- Choice → small sellers in the provinces can reach customers all over the country.

**Tiêu cực**
- Local shops close → high streets and traditional markets lose customers and character.
- Overspending → one-click payment and flash sales encourage people to make impulse purchases.
- Packaging waste → every small order comes with plastic and cardboard.

## Speaking dự đoán

**What kind of housing do you live in? (Part 1 – Home/Accommodation)**

I live in a **two-bedroom flat** in a fairly new apartment block, about twenty minutes from the city centre. It's not huge, but it's **light and airy**, and there's a small balcony where I grow a few plants. What I like most is that it's quiet at night, so I can actually **get a good night's sleep**.

**Do you get up early? (Part 1 – Morning routine)**

On weekdays I don't really have a choice, because I have to be at work by eight. I'll be honest, I usually **hit the snooze button** a couple of times, so I end up having to **grab a quick breakfast** on the way. At weekends it's a different story, I let myself **have a lie-in** until about nine.

**Do you prefer shopping online or in shops? (Part 1 – Shopping)**

It depends on what I'm buying. For everyday things like shampoo or phone accessories, I nearly always order online because it's cheaper and **saves me a trip**. But for clothes and shoes I'd rather go to a shop, because I need to **try them on** first. I've learnt the hard way that online sizes can be **wildly inaccurate**.

**Do you prefer markets or supermarkets? (Part 1 – Street markets)**

For fresh food, I'd say markets, without a doubt. The vegetables and meat at my local market are **fresher and often cheaper**, and you can **haggle a bit** if you buy a lot. Supermarkets are more convenient and hygienic, though, so I go there for things like rice, cooking oil and household items.

**What do you do when you're tired? (Part 1 – Tiredness)**

If it's just physical tiredness, I go to bed early and that **sorts it out**. But when I'm mentally drained after a long week, I find that a short walk or cooking something simple helps me **recharge my batteries** more than lying on the sofa. What I try not to do is **scroll mindlessly** on my phone, because it actually makes me feel worse.

**Why do people find it hard to keep healthy eating habits nowadays? (Part 3 – Lifestyle)**

I think the main reason is time. Most people in cities work long hours and commute, so by the evening they just want something fast, and **ready-made food** is everywhere. Delivery apps have made it even easier to **eat on the go**. On top of that, unhealthy food is often **heavily advertised**, while nobody really advertises vegetables.

**Describe a time when you received good service in a shop. (Part 2 – gợi ý dàn ý)**

- Where: a small electronics shop near the office, when a laptop charger stopped working.
- What happened: the assistant **tested it on the spot**, found the cable was faulty and replaced it under warranty.
- Extra detail: he explained how to **look after the battery**, without trying to sell anything else.
- Feeling: pleasantly surprised; **went out of his way** to help → became a regular customer.
