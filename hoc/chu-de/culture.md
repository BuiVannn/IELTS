## Cụm từ

- **cultural identity** — bản sắc văn hoá — Festivals such as Tet help Vietnamese people living abroad keep their cultural identity.
- **preserve traditional values** — giữ gìn giá trị truyền thống — Many families try to preserve traditional values by eating dinner together every evening.
- **traditional dress** — trang phục truyền thống — Most young women in Vietnam only wear traditional dress at weddings or on graduation day.
- **heavily influenced by** — (be ~) chịu ảnh hưởng mạnh từ — Teenagers' fashion choices are heavily influenced by K-pop stars and influencers.
- **cultural heritage** — di sản văn hoá — Hoi An's old town is a valuable part of our cultural heritage.
- **a lingua franca** — ngôn ngữ chung (để giao tiếp quốc tế) — English has become a lingua franca for business, science and air travel.
- **break down language barriers** — phá bỏ rào cản ngôn ngữ — Translation apps help tourists break down language barriers in local markets.
- **cultural exchange** — giao lưu văn hoá — Student exchange programmes encourage cultural exchange between Vietnam and Japan.
- **dominate the box office** — áp đảo doanh thu phòng vé — Hollywood blockbusters still dominate the box office in most Asian countries.
- **homegrown talent** — tài năng trong nước — The success of recent Vietnamese films shows that homegrown talent can compete with foreign studios.
- **lose touch with their roots** — xa rời cội nguồn — Children who grow up abroad may lose touch with their roots if they never learn their parents' language.
- **cultural diversity** — sự đa dạng văn hoá — Large cities like London are famous for their cultural diversity.
- **mass tourism** — du lịch đại trà — Mass tourism has turned some quiet villages in Sapa into crowded shopping streets.
- **the commercialisation of culture** — thương mại hoá văn hoá — The commercialisation of culture means some ethnic dances are now performed only for paying tourists.
- **respect local customs** — tôn trọng phong tục địa phương — Visitors should respect local customs, for example by covering their shoulders in temples.
- **be passed down through generations** — được truyền qua nhiều thế hệ — The recipe for my grandmother's spring rolls has been passed down through generations.
- **adopt a Western lifestyle** — theo lối sống phương Tây — As incomes rise, more urban families adopt a Western lifestyle, from fast food to celebrating Christmas.
- **a uniform global culture** — một nền văn hoá toàn cầu đồng nhất — Critics fear that globalisation will create a uniform global culture where every city looks the same.
- **broaden their horizons** — mở mang tầm nhìn — Watching foreign films can help young people broaden their horizons without leaving home.

## Kho ý tưởng Task 2

### Mặc quần áo phương Tây thay vì trang phục truyền thống

Đề (01/07/2026, Two-part): People in many countries wear Western clothes rather than traditional dress. Why? Is this a positive or negative development?

**Nguyên nhân**
- Practicality → jeans and T-shirts are cheaper, easier to wash and suit modern jobs → e.g. it is hard to ride a motorbike in a long ao dai.
- Global brands and media → films and influencers set trends → e.g. Uniqlo and Zara shops are packed in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City.

**Tích cực**
- Comfort and freedom of choice → people can dress for their climate and work.
- A shared style makes international business and travel easier.

**Tiêu cực**
- Loss of cultural identity → traditional craftsmanship may disappear → e.g. fewer tailors in Hue still make hand-sewn ao dai.

> Gợi ý: kết luận cân bằng: phần lớn tích cực, miễn là trang phục truyền thống còn được mặc vào dịp lễ.

### Tiếng Anh là ngôn ngữ toàn cầu

Đề (10/05/2026, Opinion): The advantages of English as a global language will continue to outweigh the disadvantages. Agree or disagree?

**Đồng ý**
- A lingua franca for work and study → one shared language saves time and money → e.g. most research papers and software documentation are in English.
- Better job opportunities → e.g. English speakers in Vietnam often earn more in tourism, IT and foreign companies.
- Breaks down language barriers between non-native speakers → e.g. Vietnamese and Korean engineers communicate in English.

**Không đồng ý (một phần)**
- Minority languages are dying → children prefer English over their local language.
- Unfair advantage → native speakers win more contracts and university places.

### Nhập phim nước ngoài hay tự sản xuất phim

Đề (05/01 & 25/01/2026, Discussion): Some people think a country should import foreign films and TV programmes, while others think it should produce its own. Discuss both views.

**Nhập phim nước ngoài**
- Higher quality and lower cost → buying a finished series is cheaper than producing one.
- Broadens horizons → audiences learn about other cultures and languages.

**Tự sản xuất**
- Supports homegrown talent and creates jobs → e.g. several recent Vietnamese family dramas have earned hundreds of billions of dong at the box office.
- Protects cultural identity → local stories, history and humour reach young viewers.
- Without quotas, Hollywood would dominate the box office completely.

> Gợi ý: kết luận: nên làm cả hai, có hạn ngạch hợp lý cho phim nội địa.

### Bảo tàng nên tập trung vào văn hoá nước mình

Đề (25/05/2026; 08/02, Opinion): Museums and art galleries should focus on their own country's history and culture rather than foreign ones. Agree or disagree?

**Đồng ý**
- Preserve cultural heritage → museums are the main place where young people learn national history → e.g. the Museum of Ethnology in Hanoi.
- Attracts foreign tourists who want to see something unique.

**Không đồng ý**
- Cultural exchange → foreign exhibitions build understanding and tolerance.
- Local history is connected to world history → e.g. Vietnam's past cannot be explained without China, France and the US.

### Giao lưu quốc tế và mất bản sắc dân tộc

Đề (04/08/2026, Discussion): More business and cultural contact between countries is a positive development vs. it causes a loss of national identity. Discuss both views.

**Tích cực**
- Economic growth → trade and foreign investment create jobs → e.g. electronics factories in Bac Ninh employ tens of thousands of local workers.
- Cultural exchange → people become more open-minded and tolerant.

**Mất bản sắc**
- A uniform global culture → the same chains, songs and films everywhere.
- Young people adopt a Western lifestyle and lose touch with their roots → e.g. some teenagers know Halloween better than the Mid-Autumn Festival.

### Du lịch và văn hoá địa phương

Đề (đề quen thuộc, Adv–Disadv): International tourism is growing fast. Do the advantages for local culture outweigh the disadvantages?

**Lợi ích**
- Money for preservation → ticket income pays to restore old buildings → e.g. Hue Imperial City.
- Revives traditional crafts → tourists buy handmade products → e.g. Bat Trang pottery villages.

**Bất lợi**
- The commercialisation of culture → rituals become shows for paying tourists.
- Mass tourism damages heritage sites and pushes up local prices → e.g. overcrowding in Hoi An at weekends.

## Speaking dự đoán

**Do you prefer local or foreign films? (Part 1 – Films/Cinema)**

To be honest, I watch **far more foreign films**, mainly Hollywood and Korean ones, because the **production quality** is usually higher. That said, Vietnamese cinema has **come a long way** recently, and some local comedies are really worth watching. I like that they **reflect everyday life** in a way I can easily relate to.

**What kind of clothes do you usually wear? (Part 1 – Clothes)**

Mostly **casual clothes**, like jeans and plain T-shirts, because they're comfortable and **suit the hot weather** here. For work I have to **dress a bit more smartly**, so I wear a shirt and trousers. I only wear an ao dai **on special occasions**, such as Tet or a friend's wedding.

**Do old buildings reflect a culture? (Part 1 – Old buildings)**

Yes, definitely. Old buildings show how people lived, what they believed and even what the climate was like. For example, the **French colonial villas** in Hanoi tell you a lot about **a particular period** of our history. When they're **knocked down** to make way for shopping centres, I feel we lose **a piece of our identity**.

**Do you prefer modern or traditional art? (Part 1 – Art)**

I'm more **drawn to traditional art**, especially Dong Ho woodblock prints. They're simple, but every picture **tells a story** about village life or Tet customs. Modern art can be interesting, but sometimes I **can't make sense of it** without reading the description.

**Is it easier for children to learn a language? (Part 3 – Person)**

On the whole, I think it is. Children **pick up** pronunciation naturally and they're **less afraid of making mistakes**, which adults often worry about. However, adults have **a clear purpose**, such as a job or an exam, so they can be more disciplined. In my case, I started English late, but **sheer practice** made a huge difference.
