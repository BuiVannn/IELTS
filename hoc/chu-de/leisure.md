## Cụm từ

- **take up a hobby** — bắt đầu một sở thích mới — Many people take up a hobby like running or baking to cope with stress at work.
- **get into something** — bắt đầu mê cái gì — I didn't get into photography until I bought my first decent phone.
- **wind down** — thư giãn dần, xả hơi — Listening to music on the bus home helps me wind down after work.
- **let off steam** — xả stress — Playing badminton twice a week is a good way to let off steam.
- **lose track of time** — quên cả thời gian — When I'm reading a good novel, I completely lose track of time.
- **a bit of me-time** — chút thời gian cho riêng mình — On Sunday mornings I like to have a bit of me-time before the rest of the family wakes up.
- **catch a film** — đi xem phim — We sometimes catch a film after dinner on Friday nights.
- **binge-watch** — xem liền một mạch nhiều tập — I binge-watched the whole series in one weekend, which I'm not proud of.
- **a real page-turner** — cuốn sách hấp dẫn không dứt ra được — The crime novel I read last month was a real page-turner.
- **curl up with a good book** — cuộn mình đọc sách — On rainy days, there's nothing better than curling up with a good book.
- **have a song on repeat** — nghe đi nghe lại một bài — I always have a song on repeat when I'm stuck in traffic.
- **see a band live** — xem ban nhạc biểu diễn trực tiếp — Seeing a band live is a completely different experience from listening at home.
- **a die-hard fan** — fan cứng — My cousin is a die-hard fan of Manchester United and never misses a match.
- **a couch potato** — người lười, chỉ nằm xem TV — In the cold months I turn into a bit of a couch potato.
- **stay active** — duy trì vận động — Joining a five-a-side football team is how I stay active.
- **a sense of achievement** — cảm giác thành tựu — Finishing my first 10K run gave me a real sense of achievement.
- **a creative outlet** — cách để thể hiện sự sáng tạo — Drawing is a creative outlet that has nothing to do with my office job.
- **not my cup of tea** — không phải gu của tôi — Horror films are not my cup of tea, so I usually skip them.
- **a whole new world** — cả một thế giới mới — Once you start learning the guitar, a whole new world of music opens up.

## Kho ý tưởng Task 2

### Có nên cấm thể thao mạo hiểm

Đề (11/07 & 16/07/2026; 04/03, Discussion): Some people think dangerous sports should be banned, while others believe people should be free to choose. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

**Nên cấm**
- Risk of death and serious injury → e.g. climbers and motorbike racers die every year, often in avoidable accidents.
- Cost to society → rescue teams and hospital treatment are paid for with public money.
- Bad influence → young fans copy stunts they see online without proper training.

**Tự do lựa chọn**
- Adults have the right to take risks with their own lives, as they do with smoking or driving.
- Benefits → confidence, fitness and a sense of achievement → e.g. trekking Fansipan or paragliding in Mù Cang Chải has become popular with young Vietnamese.
- Regulation is better than a ban → licences, safety equipment and qualified instructors make sports safer without removing freedom.

### Đăng cai sự kiện thể thao quốc tế

Đề (14/08/2026; 08/03, Discussion): Some say hosting international sports events is good for a country; others think it is bad. Discuss both views.

**Có lợi**
- Tourism and global image → e.g. the 2022 SEA Games in Vietnam brought visitors and media attention.
- New infrastructure → stadiums, roads and public transport remain after the event.
- National pride and interest in sport → children are inspired to stay active.

**Có hại**
- Huge costs → stadiums often stand empty afterwards → e.g. several venues from the 2004 Athens Olympics were abandoned.
- Money diverted from hospitals and schools.
- Disruption → local residents face traffic, higher prices and sometimes eviction.

### Doanh nghiệp lớn nên xây cơ sở thể thao cho cộng đồng

Đề (17/08/2026; 10/03, Opinion): Large companies should provide sports and social facilities for local communities. Agree or disagree?

**Đồng ý**
- Companies profit from the community → giving something back improves their reputation.
- Public budgets are limited → private funding can build parks, pitches and libraries faster.
- Healthier workforce → staff and their families use the facilities too.

**Không đồng ý**
- The main responsibility lies with local government, which is accountable to residents.
- Commercial motives → facilities may be covered in advertising or closed when profits fall.

> Gợi ý: đồng ý một phần là an toàn: công ty nên đóng góp, nhưng không thay thế được trách nhiệm của chính quyền.

### Nhập phim nước ngoài hay tự sản xuất

Đề (05/01 & 25/01/2026, Discussion): Some think governments should spend money on producing local films and TV programmes, while others prefer importing them. Discuss both views.

**Phim trong nước**
- Protects culture and language → local stories reflect national history and values.
- Creates jobs in the creative industry → e.g. recent Vietnamese films such as "Mai" have broken box-office records at home.

**Phim nhập khẩu**
- Cheaper and higher quality → big-budget Hollywood or Korean productions are hard to compete with.
- Opens a whole new world → audiences learn about other cultures and pick up foreign languages.
- Competition pushes local producers to raise their standards.

### Giải trí trên màn hình thay cho sở thích thực tế

Đề (đề quen thuộc, Positive–Negative): Many people now spend their free time watching videos and playing games on screens rather than doing hobbies or outdoor activities. Is this a positive or negative development?

**Tích cực**
- Cheap and accessible → anyone with a phone can wind down after a long day.
- Some online activities are educational or social → language apps, online chess with friends.

**Tiêu cực**
- Sedentary leisure → lack of exercise and poor sleep.
- Passive rather than creative → people miss the sense of achievement that comes from mastering a skill.
- Fewer real-life connections → e.g. families sitting in the same room, each on their own phone.

## Speaking dự đoán

**What kind of music do you like? (Part 1 – Music)**

I've got quite **eclectic taste**, to be honest, but I mostly listen to indie and acoustic stuff. I tend to **have one song on repeat** for a week and then get completely sick of it. When I'm working, I prefer instrumental music, because lyrics **distract me** too much.

**Do you prefer watching films alone or with friends? (Part 1 – Films)**

It depends on the film. For something **light-hearted** like a comedy, I'd rather go with friends, because laughing together is half the fun. But if it's a slow, serious drama, I actually prefer watching it on my own, so I can **really get absorbed** in the story without anyone checking their phone next to me.

**Do you watch sports on TV? (Part 1 – Sports programmes)**

Mostly football, and mostly the big tournaments. I'm not **a die-hard fan** of any club, but when the national team plays, I always watch with friends at a café, and **the atmosphere is electric**. Watching a match on your own at home just isn't the same.

**What do you do when you feel bored? (Part 1 – Feeling bored)**

My first instinct is to **reach for my phone**, but I'm trying to break that habit. These days I'll go for a walk or **flick through** a book I've left half-finished. Sometimes I just call a friend, which usually **does the trick**.

**Why do people collect things? (Part 1 – Collecting things)**

I think for a lot of people it's about **the thrill of the hunt**, finding that one rare item to complete a set. Collecting can also be quite nostalgic, like people who keep **old stamps or coins** from their childhood. And for some, it's simply a way to **take their mind off** work.

**Has technology changed how people spend their free time? (Part 3 – Day out)**

Enormously, I'd say. A generation ago, people **met up in person** and did things outdoors, whereas now a lot of leisure time is spent on screens, streaming or gaming. The upside is that hobbies are **far more accessible**: you can learn photography or the guitar from free videos. The downside is that people are **less physically active** and often more isolated.

**Describe a person you know who really likes taking photos. (Part 2 – gợi ý dàn ý)**

- Who: a former colleague, **a self-taught photographer** who started with just a phone camera.
- Where: street scenes in the Old Quarter at dawn, and landscapes on weekend trips to the mountains.
- What he/she does: gets up at 5 am **to catch the golden hour**, shares photos online, sometimes sells prints.
- Feeling: admire the patience; photography is **a creative outlet** that balances a desk job.
