# Speaking Part 1 · câu trả lời ngắn

Part 1: 3 câu, mỗi câu **khoảng 30 giây**. Không cần từ khó, chỉ cần nói **liền, rõ, đúng thì**.

## Công thức 3 bước (mọi câu)

1. **Trả lời thẳng:** *I usually… / My favourite… is… / I live in…*
2. **1 lý do:** *because… / The reason is that…*
3. **1 chi tiết hoặc ví dụ:** *For example,… / Last weekend,… / In the future, I'd like to…*

**Câu mồi (khi cần 2 giây nghĩ):** *Well,… / Let me think… / That's a good question…*
**Câu kéo dài (khi còn thời gian):** *That's why I really like it. / I hope I can… in the future.*

Các câu trả lời dưới đây dùng chung 1 "nhân vật" (làm văn phòng ở Hà Nội, có bạn thân từ đại học, từng đi Đà Lạt, đang học tiếng Anh) để khớp với 5 chuyện Part 4. Đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn.

## Bản thân, nhà cửa

**Please tell me about yourself.**
> My name is Nam, and I'm from Hanoi. I work in an office, and in my free time I'm learning English. I'm quite friendly and I love travelling, especially to the mountains.

**Where do you live?** · **Tell me about the place where you live.**
> I live in an apartment in Hanoi with my family. It's not very big, but it's comfortable and close to my office. There are many small shops and cafés near my home, so it's very convenient.

**Please describe your room.** *(có trong kho)*
> My room is small but cosy. There is a bed, a desk and a big window, so it's bright in the morning. I usually study English at my desk in the evening. It's my favourite place in the house.

**What do you like about your hometown?** · **What is your hometown famous for?**
> My hometown is famous for its food, especially pho. People there are friendly and helpful. I like it because it's quieter than the city centre, and I can relax there.

**Tell me about a place you often go to.** *(có trong kho)*
> I often go to a small coffee shop near my house. It's quiet and the coffee is very good. I usually go there at the weekend to read or meet my friends.

**Tell me about a famous place in your country.**
> One famous place in Vietnam is Ha Long Bay. It has thousands of small islands and green water. Many tourists visit it every year, and you can sleep on a boat there. I went there two years ago, and it was beautiful.

## Mẹo nói mượt

- **Đừng dừng giữa câu để tìm từ khó.** Không nhớ *loyal* thì nói *friendly and always with me*.
- **Nói xong 3 câu mà vẫn còn giờ:** thêm *That's why I really like it.* hoặc *I hope I can… in the future.*
- **Câu hỏi về quá khứ** (*last weekend, last time, first school*): dùng **quá khứ đơn** cho cả 3 câu.
- **Câu hỏi về thói quen** (*usually, free time*): dùng **hiện tại đơn**, nhớ thêm **s/es** sau *he/she/it*.
